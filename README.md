# EMS Sandbox

An interactive, browser-based visualization of the electromagnetic spectrum.
No download. No install. Just open and explore.

## What It Is

The electromagnetic spectrum spans 24 orders of magnitude — from radio waves
the size of continents to gamma rays smaller than an atomic nucleus. Almost
none of it is visible to the human eye. All of it is happening around you
right now.

EMS Sandbox makes it tangible.

## Tiers

**Tier I — The Full Spectrum**
A logarithmic spectrum bar spanning the complete range from 3 Hz to 3×10²⁴ Hz.
Drag your cursor across it and live readouts update for band, frequency,
wavelength, and photon energy. Audio follows your position — the visible range
maps to mid-range piano tones, infrared drops toward inaudible bass, ultraviolet
climbs toward a whistle, gamma goes silent. Click ENABLE in the audio bar first.

**Tier II — The Seven Bands**
Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-Ray, Gamma. Select any band
for a full detail panel — frequency range, wavelength, energy levels, natural
sources, real-world applications, and a characteristic audio tone for that band.

**Tier III — Wave Physics**
A live animated electromagnetic wave showing E and B fields in real time.
Adjustable frequency, amplitude, and polarization angle. AM, FM, and interference
modes. Audio tracks the frequency slider — change the wave, hear it change.

**Tier IV — 3D Sandbox**
A full 3D electromagnetic wave. Drag to rotate on any axis. Switch transmission
medium between vacuum, air, water, and glass and watch the live data panel update
carry distance, penetration, and attenuation in real time. AM, FM, and PM
modulation. Power, wavelength, and period calculated live.

## Audio Note
The audio system represents spectrum position as audible tone — not the actual
electromagnetic frequency, which no human ear can detect. It is a conceptual
mapping: what the spectrum would feel like if it were sound. A volume slider
and mute control are in the audio bar. Click ENABLE once on page load to
unlock audio (required by browser autoplay policy).

## Controls
- **Void / Bio toggle** — dark and light themes
- **ENABLE** — unlocks audio (click once per session)
- **Volume slider** — master audio level
- **Tier buttons** — navigate between exploration modes
